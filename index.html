Operating principles

Designed for systems that cannot rely on hope.

Probabilistic models need deterministic boundaries. The product work is organized around explicit policy, local ownership, human authority and evidence that survives scrutiny.

◇

Deterministic control

Critical decisions are governed by rules and contracts—not model confidence alone.

◎

Local ownership

Data, context and operational state remain under the operator’s control.

⌁

Human authority

High-impact actions can be stopped, reviewed or approved in context.

▣

Verifiable evidence

Decisions and incidents leave inspectable records rather than unverifiable claims.
