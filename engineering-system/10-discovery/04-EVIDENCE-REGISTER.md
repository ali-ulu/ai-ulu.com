# Evidence Register

Canonical research provenance ledger.

For every EVID-### capture:
- claim supported/challenged
- source title/URL or internal source
- source type
- publication date when available
- access date
- evidence summary
- reliability assessment
- confidence
- jurisdiction/population/version where relevant
- affected NEED/REQ/NFR/ADR/RISK
- expiry/revalidation date for fast-changing facts

Other research documents reference EVID IDs instead of duplicating evidence tables.
