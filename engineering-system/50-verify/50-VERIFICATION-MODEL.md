# Verification Model

Verification evaluates implementation against the agreed engineering contract.

Pipeline:
Automated Checks → AI Review → Risk/Confidence → Human Review Decision → Final Record

Modes:
- AI: low-risk, rapid
- Human: independent expert review
- Hybrid: AI scans broadly, human reviews material/high-risk areas

Suggested routing:
LOW → automated + AI
MEDIUM → AI + human sampling
HIGH → AI + full human review
CRITICAL → AI + multiple/independent human approvals as defined

Verification checks requirements, specifications, architecture, security, tests and execution-contract compliance.
