# Launch / Deploy Handoff

Launch add-on may include:
- production environment setup
- domain/DNS/TLS
- runtime/database/storage
- secrets/configuration
- migrations
- CI/CD
- monitoring/error logging
- backups and restore test
- health checks
- rollback verification
- distribution/store submission as applicable
- operational owner
- known limitations

Approve launch only when Definition of Production is satisfied or exceptions are explicitly accepted.
