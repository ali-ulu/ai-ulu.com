# Origin / Idea Preservation

The system must not silently lose these capabilities:
- discovery before specification;
- market, competitor, repository/implementation and literature/domain research;
- technical, economic, operational, security, time, platform and dependency feasibility;
- evidence states: evidence-backed, hypothesis, customer-confirmed, unknown;
- requirements analysis for ambiguity, contradiction, gaps, duplicates, impossible requirements, assumptions and edge cases;
- YAGNI/KISS/DRY and required/optional/future/rejected/out-of-scope classification;
- product model plus user/business/data/decision/state/sequence/integration/error flows;
- data model, architecture, engineering rules and dependency governance;
- complexity-aware decomposition and dependency graph;
- task generation, context-aware checklists and execution contracts;
- forward and reverse traceability;
- assumptions, ADRs, risks and technical debt;
- security integrated across the lifecycle;
- change management, baselines, deviations, human approvals, context packs and handoffs;
- reproducibility, rollback/recovery and stop conditions;
- project profiles, method selection and complexity-scaled process;
- controlled project learning;
- explicit DO-NOT-BUILD / NO-GO decisions;
- Blueprint, Build, Verify and optional Launch service packaging;
- AI, human and hybrid verification;
- future standardized reviewer marketplace and review receipts.

Files may be merged, but capabilities require an explicit ADR + approval before removal.
