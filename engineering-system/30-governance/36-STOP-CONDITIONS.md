# Stop Conditions

The agent/developer must stop and escalate when:
- essential scope is ambiguous
- requirements materially conflict
- missing evidence would force a major guess
- feasibility is UNKNOWN/NO-GO
- protected decision lacks approval
- implementation needs architecture/scope/schema/public-contract deviation
- dependency cannot be justified/reviewed
- hard security/privacy constraint would be violated
- destructive migration/data-loss risk appears unexpectedly
- required verification fails beyond tolerance
- current baseline cannot be identified
- risk threshold is exceeded

Stopping is correct behavior, not failure.
